Secure and in control

The agent can never
authorize itself.

Two rules do the heavy lifting. The model can observe and propose; it cannot approve. And a human decision only ever enters through the host boundary — never from a notification, never from a replayed ack, never from the agent itself.

Your password never enters the model's view

The vault holds a generated secret and a P-256 keypair on disk. The Semantic X-Ray overlay labels the field BLIND VAULT — hidden from LLM/JS, and the engine injects the credential itself. It never appears in an observation, a proposal, an audit record, a screenshot, or a log line.

Assertion signing: ECDSA P-256 over authenticatorData + SHA256(clientDataJSON)

Three taps, and only on irreversible things

requires_glasses is true for exactly two operations: submit and fill_secret. Everything else runs under policy. A rejected proposal is destroyed, and a replayed acknowledgement decides nothing twice.

Human decisions only: surface = glasses_dat

The adversary

Meet the agent you
never delegate to.

Walkaway assumes the agent is hostile or compromised: a hijacked portal, a spoofed notification, a replayed tap, a tampered manifest. Each of those has a named control, and each control has a test behind it.

THREAT 01

Forge an approval

received, dat_sent and dat_failed are transport receipts, not permission. Only approve or reject carrying source="glasses_dat" ever becomes a decision.

Blocked · source-bound decisions
THREAT 02

Replay your tap

A replayed acknowledgement decides nothing twice. A re-proposed submit approved from the lenses commits exactly once, and a rejected proposal is destroyed rather than left on the shelf.

Blocked · single-use decisions
THREAT 03

Tamper with the batch

Tap three releases nine submissions, so the manifest is explicit: every proposal_id, fingerprint, generation, session and portal. A tampered manifest is rejected with HTTP 409.

Blocked · bound batch manifest
THREAT 04

Steal the password

The vault is blind — hidden from the model and from page JavaScript. Secret key material and generated passwords never reach logs, proposals, audit records, screenshots or test output.

Blocked · blind vault, zero leaks
THREAT 05

Sign as you, later

Every assertion is an ECDSA P-256 signature bound to the challenge, the origin and the relying party. Wrong challenge, wrong origin, wrong RP and replayed assertions all fail verification.

Blocked · challenge-bound P-256
THREAT 06

Let it approve itself

The agent can observe and propose. It cannot approve. A decision enters only through the host boundary, from a human wearing the glasses — never from a notification, never from a replayed ack.

Blocked · human-only authority
