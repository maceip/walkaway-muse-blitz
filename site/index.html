Walkaway

Leave your desk. Your agent keeps working. Your glasses only interrupt you for decisions.

Walkaway lets Muse delegate real browser work to an agent in your own Chrome, while approval for every step happens on Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, where the wearer sees the live screenshot and the exact proposed action. The Muse Connector can start, propose, monitor, reject, and stop tasks. The connector has no approve tool: Muse can never authorize Muse's own browser actions.
