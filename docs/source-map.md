Distinct from `apps/marketing-site/`, which is the Walkaway product surface; the two do not share a component, a generator, or a deployment. **This is the only implementation of the red team page** — the earlier no-build static site (`apps/ai-red-team-site/`) was retired on 2026-11-23; extend this app instead of adding a second one. **Media pipeline:** `scripts/build-redteam-media.py` is the single generator for the page's orb media and scene manifest; it reads `videos/scenes.json` (the config: which 14 scenes exist, which is the default, which of the delivered sources carried a storm to take off, which scene a film loops, and how much of a source to crop in before staging it) and the source media in `videos/` (the inbox; only the media a scene names is shipped, and the pipeline prunes what it used to own — the inbox also keeps the superseded sources for the record: `city-globe-scan-bokeh.mp4`, and `city-scan-720.mp4` / `city-redteam-720.mp4`, the 720x720 24 fps versions of the two clips the owner re-delivered at 1080x1080 60 fps), and writes `public/media/scenes/<id>.{png,webp}` (the globe, masked to the circle inscribed in the canonical square), `public/video/<id>.mp4` (the same square), the generated manifest `src/media/scenes.ts`, and `media-hashes.json`. Never hand-edit the shipped media or the manifest — regenerate them. **The canonical stage is 1024x1024** (`CANVAS`): the delivery border is cropped and the frame is Lanczos-resized onto it, and every scene is built at that size, which is what makes the globe the same size and in the same place in every scene — so the page can cross from one to the next without the globe jumping. **The storm is removed** for the scenes whose delivered source carried it (`"repair_storm": true`): the cloud is found as the bright, washed-out, smooth mass in the top band of the frame, and the hole it leaves is filled with the sky of that row, taken from the dark pixels in the middle of the frame so the bright city below cannot leak into it. The lightning in it is a violet blob and the city's own magenta light is in thin streaks, and the two are told apart by shape (the blob fills 0.35 of its own bounding box, the streaks 0.17). **The storm film is the one source that keeps its storm** — it is the storm, and it is one deliberate beat in the cycle. **Every film is slowed to a third of the speed it was delivered at**, the frames interpolated up to the rate the slowed film needs so the camera glides rather than juddering and their timestamps stretched to match, or the film would be the same length and simply dropping frames; the owner's three new clips are slowed a half speed instead, because they are already smooth and slow and a third of their speed would leave the scan's dark beat on the page for three seconds. **The frame rate of a delivered clip is read, not assumed** — the pipeline used to declare every clip to be 24 fps, which would have played a 60 fps delivery two and a half times too slow; the rate now comes from the clip, the interpolation runs at that rate times the slow-down, and every film ships at 24 fps. **The cache key includes the generator's own hash**, so changing the generator rebuilds the media rather than leaving the old media in place. `scripts/build-orb-assets.py` and `apps/ai-red-team-web/scripts/sync-video.mjs` were retired on 2026-11-23: the alpha extraction they existed for is gone (the orb is clipped to a circle in CSS, so the circle is the silhouette), and the film copy step is gone (the pipeline writes the films itself). `scripts/build-space-backdrop.py` is the second generator and a separate capability: it **draws** the sky at 4096×2304 — ~46,000 faint stars on a power-law magnitude distribution, a second brighter tier of ~1,600 drawn wide enough to survive the browser's downscale as points, the galactic band, and the Andromeda galaxy — and writes `public/media/space/deep-field.webp`. It replaced two NASA survey photographs (580×498 and 550×373 px) that the page stretched to `168vmax`, which is why the sky used to read as a pasted photograph. **The sky is the only thing in the page that is space** — there is no canvas over it: `src/lib/useStarfield.ts` (a canvas painting eighty-five white dots that fell downwards and wrapped around, which on a dark page read as snowfall) was removed on 2026-11-24, and `scripts/verify-ai-red-team-web.py` fails if a canvas or that module comes back. The sphere carries **no wireframe**: the latitude/longitude grid was removed on 2026-11-23 because it made the orb read as a basketball of lines, and `src/components/Wireframe.tsx` is gone — do not bring it back. `scripts/verify-ai-red-team-web.py` is the integrity check (static: the build, the assets and the generators agree), `scripts/check-ai-red-team-web-browser.py` is the browser check (Playwright against a running build: every scene opens, the films play, the crossing is a fade, the order is shuffled, nothing overflows, and the globe answers the wheel and the click), `scripts/preview-ai-red-team-web.py` serves the build locally on 8130, and on the host the systemd unit `redteam-web.service` serves it on 127.0.0.1:8091 behind Caddy (`/etc/caddy/redteam.caddy`, tracked at `deploy/redteam.caddy`) |
